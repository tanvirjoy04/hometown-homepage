Top three activities to do at Dhaka

Explore the historical charm
of Old Dhaka

Visit Ahsan Manzil, Lalbagh Fort
for river views and Mughal
history.

Relax at Hatirjheel

Large, modern lake complex, a
beautiful escape with walkways,
cafes, and boat rides, a peaceful
contrast to the city's hustle.
Great spot for an evening
stroll or to enjoy the lights.

Culture at the National Museum
or Sonargaon (the old capital)

Get deep insights into the
nation's history and art.
